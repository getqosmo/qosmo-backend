What it does

It watches the boring, expensive parts of your life so you don’t have to.

It notices

Bills before they’re late. Renewals before they charge you. Two appointments that overlap on Friday. A registration about to expire.

It prepares

Not just an alert — the actual next step, ready to go, with the reason it thinks you need it and the records that back that up.

It asks

Then it stops. Anything that touches the world outside your machine waits for you to say yes.

Yours

It gets to know you. Nobody else gets to know you.

MyBot learns by watching what you actually do — which things you approve, when you like appointments, what you’ve corrected it about. That record is the part that makes it yours, and it never leaves your machine.

Private by construction

Your records stay on your machine.

Not “we don’t sell your data”. There is no server holding it. MyBot runs locally, and in sovereign mode it will not send anything to a model it doesn’t run itself.

$ mybot backup life.mybot

Your backup is sealed with a key nobody else holds — not us, not a cloud provider. Which also means: lose every recovery path and the data is gone. That’s what “nobody else can read it” actually costs, and we’d rather say so.

Honest limits

What MyBot will not do.

Most of this list could be quietly removed to make the product sound better. It’s here because you’re about to hand something your whole life.

01

It won’t move money. Not in this version. The plumbing exists and is deliberately switched off.

02

It won’t say “done” unless it is. If a service didn’t confirm, MyBot says it doesn’t know, and never quietly retries.

03

It won’t take instructions from your email. Anything that arrives from outside is data, never orders — including when it’s written to look like orders.

04

It won’t give itself permission. Approve something twenty times and MyBot still asks — it can only offer to make it a rule, which you create.

05

It won’t call itself unhackable. It’s software. It keeps an append-only record of everything it does so you can check.

This is V0.1, an alpha. The security architecture, the data model and the core loop are real and tested. Calendar and mail connectors are simulated by default, and money cannot move. Don’t point it at a real bank account — point it at the demo and tell us what breaks.
